# Friday recap: the weekly update to a customer's leadership

*The format I sent a CEO and VP of Sales every Friday. Claude drafted it from the week's work; I edited and sent it.*

```text
Subject: Week [N] of [total]: [the one thing that shipped]

Shipped
- [Plain-language result, then the technical term in brackets, translated.]

What's next
- [The two or three things coming, with dates.]

One decision I need from you
- [The single ask, with a recommended answer and a deadline.]

Numbers this week
- [Number] ([LIVE PULL / ESTIMATE], [source])
```

**Rules**

1. Lead with what shipped, not what I worked on.
2. Name a technical term, then translate it in the same sentence.
3. Explain any bad-looking number before they can ask about it.
4. Close finished items out loud, so nothing lingers as "in progress."
5. At most one ask. Two asks means neither gets answered.
6. Label every number.
